Services

Primary scope of work.

Nine service lines for mining and mobile maintenance. Each can be commissioned as a discrete pack or as part of a term contract.

01

Technical document writing

End-to-end drafting, review, and issue of controlled technical documents for mining and maintenance. Documents are written to the Principal’s site standards, equipment types, and document-control requirements. Work includes new documents, revision of existing packs, and alignment so that SWPs, JSAs, and service documents are consistent.

02

Safe Work Procedures (SWPs)

Task-specific procedures covering purpose, scope, competency, hazards, isolations, tooling, PPE, step-by-step method, and emergency considerations. Written for use at the job, in training, and as evidence of a documented safe system of work.

03

Job Safety Analyses (JSAs)

Task-level hazard identification, existing and additional controls, and residual risk. Prepared to support permit-to-work, pre-start discussion, and supervision, and aligned to the associated SWP where both documents are required.

04

Service Sheets

Preventive-maintenance worksheets for defined equipment and service intervals. Structured for repeatable inspection, servicing, recording of defects, and sign-off by the responsible tradesperson and supervisor.

05

Commissioning Sheets

Checklists for new, rebuilt, modified, or returned-to-service equipment. Inspection and functional checks, hold points, and formal handover sign-off so commissioning is documented and auditable.

06

Maintenance documentation

Procedures, checklists, work instructions, and supporting records required for planned maintenance of mobile and related plant. Prepared for use in the workshop and in the field.

07

Maintenance improvement projects

Structured improvement of maintenance processes and the documents that support them: review of current methods, identification of gaps, recommended changes, and issue of updated procedures so the improved method is controlled.

08

Technical writing support for mobile maintenance

Dedicated writing support to mobile maintenance functions: capturing trades knowledge, converting informal methods into controlled documents, supporting shutdown or campaign paperwork, and maintaining consistency across machine classes and work groups.

09

Training and document control

Training of users in issued SWPs, JSAs, service and commissioning documents, and related procedures. Version control, review cycles, supersession of obsolete documents, and issue in the format required by the Principal’s document-management system.
