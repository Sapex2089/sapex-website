Executive

Statement of capability

SAPEX Mining Systems and Processes Limited offers Principal organisations a local, specialist capability: controlled technical documents written from maintenance practice, suitable for workforce use, training, and tender or contract compliance.

The company is based in Tabubil, Western Province, directed by Isaac Madrino Piwen, and structured for remote-site mining operations that require documentation aligned to site standards, permit-to-work, and audit. Ok Tedi Mining Limited is a key client.

Method

How work is delivered

  1. 01Scope confirmation against equipment, tasks, and site standards
  2. 02Information gathering from OEM data, site procedures, and authorised observation
  3. 03Drafting in the Principal’s template
  4. 04Internal technical and safety review
  5. 05Client review by nominated technical and HSE reviewers
  6. 06Controlled issue and withdrawal of superseded copies
  7. 07Training against the issued revision, where scoped

Quality

Documents are issued as controlled records with number, revision, date, and approval as required by the Principal. Related SWPs, JSAs, and sheets are kept consistent.

HSE

SWPs and JSAs specify task hazards and controls. Personnel attending site comply with the Principal’s induction, isolation, permit, and PPE requirements.

Confidentiality

Client information, OEM manuals, and site procedures are not reused for other clients. SAPEX works inside the Principal’s numbering and review workflow where specified.
