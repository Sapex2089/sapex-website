Company

Who we are

SAPEX is a Papua New Guinea company providing technical document writing and maintenance-documentation services to the mining industry, with particular capability in mobile maintenance.

Incomplete or generic procedures increase the chance of injury, rework, delayed commissioning, and audit findings. SAPEX is engaged where the Principal needs documents that match the actual task, equipment, and site controls — and that remain under revision control after issue.

The company delivers contracted professional services as a defined package or as term-contract writing support to maintenance departments.

Leadership

Isaac Madrino Piwen, Director

Isaac Madrino Piwen is Director of SAPEX Mining Systems and Processes Limited and is accountable for technical delivery and contract performance.

His role on engagements includes scoping document and improvement packages against site standards; technical direction of SWPs, JSAs, service, commissioning, and maintenance documentation; quality review before issue; liaison with the Principal’s maintenance, safety, and document-control functions; and oversight of training in issued documents.

The Director remains accountable for the accuracy, completeness, and controlled issue of work produced under contract.
