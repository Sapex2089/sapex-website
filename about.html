About SAPEX Mining Systems & Processes Limited

 

Technical Documentation, Maintenance Systems and Continuous Improvement Support for the Mining Industry.

 
 
 

Who We Are

 

SAPEX Mining Systems & Processes Limited is a Papua New Guinea owned company based in Tabubil, Western Province.

 

We specialise in technical documentation, maintenance systems support, Safe Work Procedures (SWPs), Job Safety Analyses (JSAs), Service Sheets, Commissioning Sheets, Asset Management Support and Business Improvement Projects.

 
 
 

Vision

 

To be Papua New Guinea's leading provider of technical documentation and maintenance systems solutions for the mining and resources sector.

 

Mission

 

To deliver practical, safe and site-aligned documentation that improves reliability, compliance and operational performance.

 
 
 

Director

 
   
 

Isaac Madrino Piwen

 

Director | Technical Support Services

 

More than 25 years of experience in mining, maintenance, operational safety, technical writing, maintenance systems and business improvement.

 

Experience includes mobile maintenance, maintenance documentation, commissioning support, service sheets, SWPs, JSAs and condition monitoring support.
